Zhaocheng Lin1, Liusha Yang1*, Junan Zhang2, Zhizheng Wu2

01 PAPER OVERVIEW

Abstract

Generative universal speech enhancement (USE) improves speech quality under diverse and compound distortions by leveraging learned speech priors. Instead of training an enhancement-specific generative model from scratch, we propose ReConSE (Recurrent Control for Speech Enhancement), a parameter-efficient framework that repurposes a frozen pretrained F5-TTS flow-matching model for USE. ReConSE preserves the clean-speech prior and optional text-conditioning pathway of F5-TTS, while learning lightweight conditioning modules to guide clean-speech generation from degraded input. We introduce Backbone-Aware Recurrent Control, which reuses a single control block across backbone depth and updates its control state with the evolving DiT representation for effective degraded-speech conditioning. To improve linguistic fidelity under severe degradations, a Sparse Semantic Adapter injects degradation-robust semantic information into selected DiT layers. Experiments on denoising and general speech restoration show that ReConSE, trained on a smaller paired dataset with substantially fewer trainable parameters, achieves performance comparable to generative enhancement models trained from scratch.
